How to read this list

This board has more open TBD lines than its siblings — a real, honest reflection of where the design actually is: the AAOS pivot that created this board only landed 2026-09-25, and several real decisions (front-end FET/TVS exact parts, the fuse's exact rating suffix, which Verdin SKU, and 4 provisional pin-header connectors standing in for harness connectors not yet designed) were deliberately left open rather than guessed at with a fabricated-precise part number.

Active parts with a real MPN (U1/U2/U3/U4/U6/U7, Q3, D2, J4) carry provenance straight from build_schematic.py's own comments, each one traced to a real datasheet during the design pass that introduced it.

Passives are specified parametrically — value, package and the AEC-Q200 requirement — rather than pinned to one vendor.

Pricing and stock are not included.
