# Fab package

Generated by `python fab/build_fab.py` from the routed boards. It refuses to package a board that has unconnected nets or an unexpected DRC category.

Per board (`fab/brain/`, `fab/panel-tsd/`, `fab/gauges/`; display/'s last package is in `archive/display-1966/fab/`):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `<board>_fab.zip` | Gerbers (Protel extensions, X2) and Excellon drill (plated and non-plated separate). Upload this to the board house. |
| `<board>_bom.csv` | One line per orderable part: MPN, manufacturer, quantity, designators. Source for a Mouser/DigiKey cart. |
| `<board>_pos.csv` | Pick-and-place, only needed if the fab assembles. |
| `report.txt` | DRC status and counts. |

Also `fab/combined_bom.csv`: all three boards' parts in one cart-ready list (quantities per board, so multiply by the number of boards you build).

## Order settings (all boards)

Checked against JLCPCB's published multilayer capabilities (min trace/space 0.09 mm, min drill 0.15 mm, via 0.15/0.25 mm, silkscreen text at least 1.0 mm high and 0.15 mm stroke). These boards use 0.2 mm tracks and clearances (0.16 mm for the DSI pairs), 0.3/0.6 mm vias, a smallest drill of 0.2 mm, and 1.0 mm / 0.15 mm silkscreen text, so they sit inside those limits.

- **Layers: 6** (owner decision 2026-10-18, every board). Thickness 1.6 mm, stack-up **JLC06161H-3313**: outer copper 1 oz, inner 0.5 oz; 3313 prepreg 0.0994 mm (Dk 4.1) under each outer layer, 0.55 mm cores, 2116 prepreg 0.1088 mm in the middle. Layer use: F.Cu signal, In1 solid GND, In2 signal, In3 signal with the +5V (brain, panel-tsd) / +3V3 (gauges) pour, In4 solid GND, B.Cu signal. The DSI widths below are solved for this stack-up: if the board house substitutes another, re-run `review/dsi_impedance.py` with its numbers.
- Surface finish: **ENIG**. The panel connector is 0.3 mm pitch and the MCU, buck and QFN parts are 0.5 mm pitch; HASL is not recommended.
- Solder mask and silkscreen: your choice (the layout is drawn for dark mask with white silk). Black mask with white silk suits the dash.
- Impedance control: **order brain/ and panel-tsd/ with controlled impedance, 100 ohm differential +-10 %, on the 0.16 mm pairs**. brain/: the five DSI pairs (port A) and ten LVDS pairs (port B), all on F.Cu over In1, straight from the Verdin socket to the port connectors (`brain/fan_route.py`, no length difference inside a pair, DSI about 5.4 mm, LVDS about 35 mm). panel-tsd/: the panel's three DSI lanes and clock, autorouted on F.Cu over In1 and B.Cu over In4 (skew inside a pair up to 5.5 mm). `review/dsi_impedance.py`'s 2-D field solve on JLC06161H-3313 gives Z0 51.8 ohm and Zdiff 96.4 to 103.1 ohm for any gap from 0.25 to 1.2 mm: the pairs are loosely coupled over a 0.1 mm dielectric, so the impedance hardly depends on the gap, and a pair whose legs separate stays near 100 ohm. Ask the house to confirm the width with its own calculator and to add a coupon.
- Quantity: 5 of each is the usual minimum.

## What is still unconfirmed (could force a respin of brain/ or panel-tsd/)

1. panel-tsd/: the panel FPC connector (Hirose FH26-39S-0.3SHW) is inferred from the panel datasheet; the datasheet names no connector. Ask TSD (draft in `docs/rfq-drafts.md`).
2. panel-tsd/: the panel's +/-6.5 V pin polarity is contradictory in its datasheet (table: pins 7-8 VSP, 10-11 VSN; drawing: the reverse); the board has open solder jumpers JP1/JP2 for either answer, and the panel gets no bias until one is bridged. **Before bridging, check the real panel with a multimeter's diode test, panel unplugged and unpowered**: the ICNL9707 driver's VSP pin is rated -0.3 V minimum and its VSN pin +0 V maximum (Chipone datasheet table 4-1), so each has a protection diode the other way. Red probe on a GND pin (1, 6, 9), black on pin 7: a diode drop (about 0.4 to 0.8 V) means pins 7-8 are VSP; then red on pin 10, black on GND should also read a drop (VSN). The reverse readings mean the drawing is right. If both directions read open or both read a drop, do not power it: ask TSD. The rails are set to +5.98 V and -6.05 V (2026-10-19), inside the driver's 4.5-6.5 V operating range with margin to its 6.6 V absolute maximum.
3. brain/: the Verdin wake-pin polarity is unstated; the circuit assumes a software-selectable edge.
4. brain/: USB-C receptacle J17 (GCT USB4105-GF-A): its four ground pads sit 0.194 mm from its two NPTH locating pegs (0.25 mm KiCad default; this is GCT's own land pattern). Check the board house's NPTH-to-copper rule or run its DFM check. DRC reports it as `hole_clearance` x4 (accepted category).
5. brain/: the TAC5112-Q1 audio codec, the USB switch and the audio/USB connectors are drawn from datasheets but untested; see docs/audio-and-usb.md for the list. J16 is a DNP 1x6 header for an Apple MFi coprocessor daughterboard (no Apple part chosen).

6. brain/ and panel-tsd/: the screen-port ribbon (Hirose FH12 connectors, `docs/screen-ports.md`). Confirm Hirose's per-contact rating (the design assumes 0.5 A) and which side the FH12's contacts are on, then choose the FFC (same-side or opposite-side contacts) that joins contact n to contact n between the two boards, and check that with a meter before plugging a panel in.
7. brain/: that the Verdin iMX95 drives the DSI and LVDS ports as two independent screens at once (the i.MX 95 can; question 9 in `docs/rfq-drafts.md` section 2). A single-screen car is not affected.

gauges/ has no open hardware question other than the AutoMeter sender wiring (assumed harness-powered, signal and ground only).

## Parts

Every active part and connector has a real orderable part number. Passives come from `tools/passive_catalog.py`; entries marked PATTERN in the BOM follow the manufacturer's numbering scheme with neighbouring parts verified, and should be confirmed in the distributor cart. Live pricing and stock are not included.
