How to read this list

No line is left as a placeholder part. J2/J3 are the real harness connectors (power with a sealed DTM06-3S pigtail; the private CAN link to gauges/ as a short in-dash KK 254 cable), J8/J9 are bench-only headers; the harness is decided in docs/harness.md. Still unknown: the car-side tap and the final routing. Everything else that started as an open item (the front-end FET pair, the input TVS, the fuse's exact rating, and the Verdin SoM's exact SKU) was resolved to a real, cited part on 2026-09-28. Also open, and flagged in the J4 and panel lines: the panel datasheet's table and drawing disagree on which FPC pins carry +6.5V vs -6.5V (JP1/JP2 are left open until resolved), and its FPC connector is inferred, not named.

Active parts with a real MPN carry provenance straight from build_schematic.py's own comments, each one traced to a real datasheet during the design pass that introduced it.

Passives are specified parametrically — value, package and the AEC-Q200 requirement — rather than pinned to one vendor.

Pricing and stock are not included.
