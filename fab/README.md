# Fab package

Generated by `python fab/build_fab.py` from the routed boards. It refuses to package a board that has unconnected nets or an unexpected DRC category.

Per board (`fab/display/`, `fab/gauges/`):

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `<board>_fab.zip` | Gerbers (Protel extensions, X2) and Excellon drill (plated and non-plated separate). Upload this to the board house. |
| `<board>_bom.csv` | One line per orderable part: MPN, manufacturer, quantity, designators. Source for a Mouser/DigiKey cart. |
| `<board>_pos.csv` | Pick-and-place, only needed if the fab assembles. |
| `report.txt` | DRC status and counts. |

Also `fab/combined_bom.csv`: both boards' parts in one cart-ready list (quantities per board, so multiply by the number of boards you build).

## Order settings (both boards)

Checked against JLCPCB's published 4-layer capabilities (min trace/space 0.09 mm, min drill 0.15 mm, via 0.15/0.25 mm, silkscreen text at least 1.0 mm high and 0.15 mm stroke). These boards use 0.2 mm tracks and clearances, 0.3/0.6 mm vias, a smallest drill of 0.2 mm, and 1.0 mm / 0.15 mm silkscreen text, so they sit inside those limits.

- Layers: 4. Thickness: 1.6 mm. Outer copper 1 oz, inner copper 0.5 oz.
- Surface finish: **ENIG**. The panel connector is 0.3 mm pitch and the MCU, buck and QFN parts are 0.5 mm pitch; HASL is not recommended.
- Solder mask and silkscreen: your choice (the layout is drawn for dark mask with white silk). Black mask with white silk suits the dash.
- Impedance control: not requested. The MIPI DSI pairs on display/ are 108-121 mm long with up to about 13 mm skew inside a pair (about 90 ps). At this panel's roughly 300 Mbit/s per lane (3.3 ns bit time) that is far below the MIPI skew limit, so uncontrolled impedance is acceptable for a prototype; the long run is a layout consequence of the larger power parts and is worth shortening in a later spin.
- Quantity: 5 of each is the usual minimum.

## Board sizes

- display/: about 121 x 106 mm.
- gauges/: 447 x 43 mm. Check the board house's maximum length for your chosen service (the long side is under 500 mm).

## What is still unconfirmed (could force a respin of display/)

1. The panel FPC connector (Hirose FH26-39S-0.3SHW) is inferred from the panel datasheet; the datasheet names no connector. Ask TSD (draft in `docs/rfq-drafts.md`).
2. The panel's +/-6.5 V pin polarity is contradictory in its datasheet; the board has open solder jumpers JP1/JP2 for either answer.
3. The Verdin wake-pin polarity is unstated; the circuit assumes a software-selectable edge.

gauges/ has no open hardware question other than the AutoMeter sender wiring (assumed harness-powered, signal and ground only).

## Parts

Every active part and connector has a real orderable part number. Passives come from `tools/passive_catalog.py`; entries marked PATTERN in the BOM follow the manufacturer's numbering scheme with neighbouring parts verified, and should be confirmed in the distributor cart. Live pricing and stock are not included.
