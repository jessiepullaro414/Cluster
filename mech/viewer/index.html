Cluster dash, assembly v2

The three routed boards from their KiCad models in a housing shaped after the stock 1966 five-dial cluster: gauge faces square to the driver on a dash that leans back toward the bottom, so the chrome trim is deep at the bottom to meet it (see Side); a rolled chrome edge with a black inner wall, black grained side panels with a chrome bead, chrome bezel collars, and the speedometer in a chrome cup standing out through the trim top and bottom. Checked solid against solid; no clashes left.

Fixed on gauges/ since v1

The fuel screen no longer hits anything fixed

J1 (power terminal block, 13.8 mm) and F1 (fuse holder, 7.4 mm) moved to the top edge of the board, above the screens: 15.6 mm and 12.2 mm clear of the fuel screen now. The tallest part under any screen is 2.3 mm, with 4 mm of room.

All four screen cables reach fixed

The FPC connectors moved to the board's bottom edge, 11 mm below each screen centre, opening down toward the tail. The 18.3 mm tail folds under the module and goes 3.25 mm into the 4.5 mm connector body. No screen rotation or software flip needed.

Turn arrows behind factory-style windows

The two turn LEDs moved up to where the stock indicator windows are, 29 mm above the small gauges, with a light pipe to a green lens in a chrome frame.

Still open

gauges/ is wider than the inner opening

447.1 mm against the 441.09 mm inner opening (it is 65.6 mm tall now). The housing needs 456.6 mm across, 0.3 mm a side inside the 457.2 mm outer opening.

Check: how deep the 457.2 mm outer step is on the real dash. If it is shallower than 20 mm the board needs trimming to 441 mm.

Everything else fits

The centre panel's 69.8 mm FPC reaches panel-tsd/ with 8.6 mm spare. The screen-port ribbon needs about 106 mm. The tub, square to the driver, crosses the leaning dash at an angle: 114.5 mm tall, 116.1 mm in the dash's plane, inside the 116.69 mm opening. 56 mm deep behind the panels (stock about 60 mm); in front, the trim crown stands 9 mm out and the speedometer lip 22 mm.

The trim leaves the screens readable

The speedometer's whole 101.5 mm face stays in view up to 32° off-axis past the flared throat and lip; each small face up to 28° past its bezel. The trim's crown is only 9 mm proud, so it takes nothing more.

What is real and what is assumed

Real: the board models (kicad-cli STEP), the TSD outline (110 mm body, 7.8 mm, tab to 58.49 mm), the Raystar outline (50.2 mm lens, 37.74 mm backlight, 3.99 mm), the Verdin module and standoff heights, the dash opening.
