# display/: audio, USB host and the wireless radio (added 2026-10-16)

The owner decided that wireless Android Auto and CarPlay are required and that the centre display needs sound. Review finding at the time: `display/` had only a bench USB header (J13), no codec or microphone, and the Verdin WB radios need only antennas. This page records what was added, how to wire it, and what is still unverified. Everything is generated by the scripts in `display/` (never hand-edit the KiCad files).

**Licensing is not solved by any of this.** Wireless CarPlay needs Apple's MFi programme (an authentication coprocessor and a licence); Android Auto on AAOS has Google's receiver terms. That is plan item B5 in `software/PLAN.md`. The hardware here only removes the physical reasons for failing.

## 1. Audio: TAC5112-Q1 codec

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Codec | TI **TAC5112-Q1** (orderable TAC5112WQRTVRQ1), AEC-Q100 Grade 1, WQFN-32 5x5 mm. Datasheet SLASFC2A (March 2025) read in full; pin table in `display/parts.py` (`TAC5112_Q1`) with the datasheet's own GOTCHAs |
| Why not fascia's PCM3168A-Q1 | It needs a 3.0-3.6 V digital supply (VOH 2.4 V against the module's 2.1 V absolute maximum), so fascia carries two SN74AXC4T245-Q1 translators, a two-FET I2C translator and a 3.3 V logic rail. The TAC5112-Q1's IOVDD runs at 1.8 V, so I2S and I2C go straight to the module: 1 IC, no shifters, no MCLK (its PLL locks to BCLK). It also has two DAC and two ADC channels, which is all this needs (two in, one used) |
| Supplies | IOVDD = the existing +1V8 rail. AVDD = a second TLV767-Q1 (U10, 3.3 V from +5V, 316 k / 100 k divider = 3.33 V, enabled by PWR_EN_MOCI like the 1.8 V rail). 3.3 V because at AVDD 1.8 V the line output halves to 1 Vrms and MICBIAS tops out at 1.375 V |
| Control | Verdin I2C_1 (X1 pin 12 SDA, 14 SCL), I2C address **0x50** (ADDR to ground). 2.2 k pull-ups to +1V8 (the Verdin datasheet lists no pull-ups on I2C_1; confirm on the module) |
| Audio bus | Verdin I2S_1 in controller mode: pin 30 BCLK, 32 SYNC, 34 D_OUT (to codec DIN), 36 D_IN (from codec DOUT). Pin 38 MCLK is unused. The codec is the target |
| Channels | OUT1 = left, OUT2 = right, differential. IN1 = microphone. IN2, GPIO1/2, GPO1, GPI1 unused (open) |

### Software has to do this (datasheet section 8.3)
1. Wait 2 ms after power-up (the supply ramps slower than 0.1 V/us; leave 100 ms between a power-down and the next power-up).
2. The first I2C write must set `IOVDD_IO_MODE` (P0_R2 bit 1) = 1 for 1.8 V I/O. AVDD is 3.3 V so `AVDD_MODE` stays 0.
3. Wake from sleep, set the output and input configuration, and enable MICBIAS (`MICBIAS_PDZ`, P0_R120 bit 5). The default MICBIAS value 2.75 V is right for an electret on 3.3 V AVDD. Maximum 5 mA.
4. Keep the module's I2S pins quiet (valid levels, not toggling) while the supplies sequence. The module's I2S pins reset to pull-down.

### Line-out connector J14 (JST XH, 5 pins)
| Pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | Left + |
| 2 | Left - |
| 3 | Right + |
| 4 | Right - |
| 5 | Ground (cable shield) |

Each leg passes 100 ohm, a 4.7 uF DC-blocking capacitor and the TPD4E05U06-Q1 ESD array. Full scale is 2 Vrms differential into 600 ohm or more (datasheet Table 8-1).

**Using it with an ordinary unbalanced stereo AUX input:** wire L+ to the AUX left centre pin, R+ to the right centre pin, pin 5 to the shared sleeve/ground, and **leave L- and R- unconnected**. Never tie them to ground: they are driven outputs. This gives about 1 Vrms per channel, the usual AUX level. For a balanced input (an amplifier with differential line inputs) wire all four and the shield. Keep the cable short and twisted; shield the drain at this end only.

### Microphone connector J15 (JST XH, 3 pins)
| Pin | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1 | MIC+ (bias and signal, 2.2 k from MICBIAS) |
| 2 | MIC- (return, 100 ohm to ground on the board) |
| 3 | Shield (ground) |

A 2-wire electret capsule (for example 6 mm, 2 V bias, about 0.5 mA). Both legs AC-couple (1 uF) into IN1P / IN1M so the return tap cancels ground noise. Use shielded twisted pair with the shield tied only at the board. The JST XH family is deliberately different from the KK 254 headers used for CAN (J3) and the dial (J11), so audio cannot be mated to those.

### Placement and noise
The codec cell (AUDIO, with its LDO) is in the left strip beside the Verdin socket, top-aligned, which keeps it as far as the floorplan allows from the panel-bias and backlight converters in the bottom strip: measured on the routed board, the codec is 31 mm from the nearest converter inductor (L2) and the nearest part of the whole audio block (the 3.3 V LDO's feedback resistor) is 9 mm from the backlight driver U7, directly below the connector/ESD cell (AOUT). It is not far from the DIAL ADC network either. This is distance, not isolation: the codec's analog ground is the board GND plane (In1.Cu) like everything else, as TI's layout guidance asks (star-connect all ground pins to the plane). Judge real noise on the bench with a switching-converter load before trusting the 105 dB figure.

## 2. USB-C host port (J17) on Verdin USB_2

* **Port**: Verdin `USB_2` (X1 pins 181 D-, 183 D+, 185 EN, 187 OC#), the host-only port. `USB_1` (OTG, recovery) stays on the bench header J13. USB 2.0 only: the SuperSpeed lanes stay unused (bank E, no-connect). A phone or a projection dongle negotiates USB 2.0 on this receptacle.
* **Connector**: GCT USB4105-GF-A, USB Type-C 2.0 16-pin receptacle, on the bottom board edge (footprint drawn for this part; its edge line is 3.1 mm below the origin and the floorplan aligns it to the board edge). The receptacle is board-mount; reach a console or glovebox with a panel-mount USB-C extension cable.
* **VBUS**: TPS2557-Q1 (U11, orderable TPS2557QDRBRQ1; EN is active-HIGH on this variant, do not substitute the -2556). R(ILIM) = 61.9 k gives 1.61 / 1.805 / 1.98 A min/typ/max (datasheet table). 100 k pull-down on EN keeps the port off until software enables it (the module datasheet lists a reset pull-down for USB_1_EN but none for USB_2_EN). FAULT goes to `USB_2_OC#` through a 100 k pull-up to +1V8. 5 V TVS (D8, SMAJ5.0A) on VBUS because a hot-plugged cable can ring above the switch's 7 V absolute maximum. 100 uF hybrid polymer on VBUS (USB 2.0 cites 120 uF per downstream port; the board uses the one polymer part it already carries, 17 % under).
* **VBUS pins**: only the A9/B4 pair of VBUS contacts is wired (the A4/B9 pair is on its own net, USB2_VBUS_L). The autorouter could not cross the D+/D-/CC escape to join the pairs, and every Type-C plug ties all four of its VBUS pins together, so either pair carries the current: 1.5 A across two contacts, against 1.25 A per contact in the Type-C spec. This departs from "connect all four": a 3 A port, or a stricter reading, needs the link added (B.Cu through two vias above the pad row).
* **CC**: 22 kohm Rp on CC1 and CC2 to +5V advertises **1.5 A** (USB Type-C table: 56 k default, 22 k 1.5 A, 10 k 3 A). 1.5 A rather than 3 A because the 5 V buck is shared with the whole carrier. No PD, no alternate mode; SBU unused.
* **ESD**: TPD4E05U06-Q1 on D+, D-, CC1, CC2. The shield goes straight to ground.
* **Routing**: D+/D- are routed by the autorouter, short (the port is next to the module's USB_2 pins) but not length-matched or impedance-controlled. Acceptable for a short USB 2.0 hop; check with a USB eye test if a dongle is flaky.
* **Why it exists**: a wired phone, and a wireless-projection dongle (the common Android Auto / CarPlay wireless adapters) as the fallback if native wireless cannot be licensed.

## 3. Wireless radio (Verdin iMX95 WB)
The WB module carries a **u-blox MAYA-W260-00B**: Wi-Fi 6 (2.4 and 5 GHz), simultaneous station + access point + P2P, Bluetooth 5.4 and 802.15.4, **two U.FL antenna connectors** on the module (Verdin iMX95 datasheet sections 1.7.14 and 5.18). No carrier circuit is needed. Its SDIO and Bluetooth UART use SD3 and UART pins on the SODIMM; this board does not use any of those pins.

Antenna rules for the mechanical design (none of this is drawn on the board; it is a checklist):
* **The module's U.FL connectors need cable access.** The datasheet text does not say where on the module they sit: read the Toradex mechanical drawing and keep the cables clear. The module keepout already forbids carrier parts under the module (Toradex: the whole height under the module is reserved), so the area near them is free of carrier parts; do not add tall parts or a shield can beside the socket later.
* **Antennas must not sit behind metal.** A metal dash panel, a steel bracket or the display's own frame detunes them and kills range. Use the Toradex pre-certified antenna and cable list (developer.toradex.com, "Wi-Fi cables and antennas for Toradex modules") and mount the antennas on a plastic part or through a plastic window, with the two spaced apart.
* Wireless Android Auto and CarPlay both use Bluetooth to pair and Wi-Fi to carry the picture; the two radios share the antennas, so range matters.

## 4. Reserved: Apple MFi coprocessor access point (J16, DNP, TBD)
Wireless CarPlay (iAP2) needs Apple's MFi authentication coprocessor, which is only available through Apple's MFi programme. J16 is a **do-not-populate 1x4 pad set** that reserves the connections, and no Apple part number is chosen or invented:

| J16 pin | Net | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I2C1_SDA | the shared I2C_1 bus (X1 pin 12, with the codec at 0x50). The open-source LIVI receiver probes the coprocessor at 0x10 and 0x11 (its iap2-mfi crate, read 2026-10-17), so no clash is expected; Apple's documentation is still the authority |
| 2 | I2C1_SCL | X1 pin 14 |
| 3 | MFI_RST_N | Verdin GPIO_2, X1 pin 208 (1.8 V). Reset polarity and level are TBD from Apple's MFi documentation |
| 4 | GND | |

The coprocessor's supply voltage is also TBD: it is not on this header. When the part is chosen it needs its supply, any I2C level shifting, and its own footprint (a respin or a daughter board).

## 5. Board size
The audio and USB blocks add **17.4 mm of width and no height**: display/ goes from 151.9 x 101.8 mm to **169.3 x 101.8 mm**. The bottom strip sets the width (the USB-C cell, 24 mm wide, sits in it). The audio codec cell and its LDO live in a new left-strip column beside the socket, and the connector/ESD cell (AOUT) leads the top strip, both inside the old width. Two cells were deliberately given more room around their anchor part (the USB-C receptacle 3.5 mm, the codec 3.5 mm, instead of 1.6 mm) because the first, tighter versions would not route.

Layer note: the autorouter now also gets a **GND pour on the back layer (B.Cu)** in addition to the In1 GND and In2 +5V pours. In the dense audio block, the 1 mm clearance holes of many signal vias split In1 into islands and left GND vias unconnected; the back pour ties the islands back together.

## 6. What is not verified
* **Antenna U.FL location** on the module (not in the datasheet text).
* **TAC5112-Q1**: datasheet read, not bench-tested; the DC level on the line outputs and the exact software bring-up are untested. `AVDD_MODE`/`IOVDD_IO_MODE` rules are from section 8.3.
* **Verdin I2C_1 pull-ups** (none listed) and the **GPIO expander rail** for `USB_2_EN` / `USB_2_OC#` (assumed 1.8 V like all Verdin I/O).
* **J17 hole clearance**: the four ground shield-side pads are 0.194 mm from the NPTH locating pegs (KiCad default 0.25 mm; that is GCT's own land pattern as drawn in KiCad's footprint). Confirm the board house's NPTH-to-copper limit.
* **SMAJ5.0A**'s AEC-Q101 listing and the **JST XH** ordering suffixes (the BOM marks both PATTERN).
* **Only one VBUS contact pair is wired** on J17 (see section 2).
* **USB VBUS capacitor** is 100 uF, under the USB 2.0 120 uF figure.
* **Licensing** (Apple MFi, Google receiver terms): separate business gate B5.
