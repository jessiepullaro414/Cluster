How to read this list

Active parts and connectors carry real manufacturer part numbers wherever build_schematic.py's own comments state one - that provenance lives in the schematic generator, not invented here.

A "Needs real part selection" section is real, not an error. The 12V front-end TVS/reverse-protection FETs' exact manufacturer and the S32K144's own exact memory/package order suffix were never pinned down to a single orderable P/N in this project's design passes - they're flagged here rather than given a fabricated-precise part number.

Passives are specified parametrically — value, package and the AEC-Q200 requirement — rather than pinned to one vendor, the same real reasoning ecu-pcb's own BOM uses.

Pricing and stock are not included. Nothing here was checked against a distributor's live catalogue.
